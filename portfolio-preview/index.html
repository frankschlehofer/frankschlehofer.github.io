About Me

Passionate about software development, machine learning, and creating innovative solutions that make a difference. Currently pursuing my BS and MS in Computer Science at Purdue University.

Ever since my high school computer science teacher opened my eyes to the endless possibilities within tech, I've been captivated. What excites me most is the sheer versatility of it all – the ability to create a tool that makes my mom's life easier with an app for her fridge, and then pivot to exploring something as intricate as a hand-tracking model.

I'm someone who is genuinely driven to succeed in whatever I take on, always aiming for accuracy and a complete solution. But I also know that the best results often come from teamwork. I truly enjoy collaborating and find I do my best work in an open, honest team setting where everyone feels comfortable asking for help and sharing ideas. My current focus is on building out full-stack applications, like my Fridge app, where I'm getting hands-on experience with everything from UI development to API integration and database management.

Beyond the screen, my friends and family are a big part of my life and often the inspiration behind my projects. I also love staying active with weightlifting and basketball, unwinding with a bit of Minecraft, or simply being outdoors.

I'm eager to connect with people who are passionate about building innovative and meaningful technology. Let's chat!
